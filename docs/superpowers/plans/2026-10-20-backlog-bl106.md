# Backlog bl106: the coordinator lanes report a registry they could not list

**Issue:** #534 (ARCH-08b, after ARCH-08). **Size:** M. **Deploy class:** server lane only; no `ccd/`, `agent/` or `pwa/` byte, no wire change. One `FleetIO` member is removed (`readFileB64`, no callers), and none is added.

## Problem

Five registry listings in the coordinator lanes read the plain `io.readdir`, which answers `null` on ANY failure, and wrapped it in `catch { names = null; }`:

- `coord/collectTempRoot.ts`: `readCollectListing` (the `tmproots/` and `tmpquarantine/` population) and `collectSwitches`;
- `coord/endDeadCoordinator.ts`: `stillCrashed` and the act's switches;
- `coord/expireArchived.ts`: `expirySwitches`.

Each one already stopped on a `null` listing. But the stop could not say WHY the registry did not list: a missing directory, a directory this box cannot read, and an agent fault all read the same. `readdirMeasured` (D-4636) tells them apart with the existing `ReadFailure` words (`absent`, `unreadable`). Separately, the `FleetIO` members stated their failure behaviour only in trailing `// null on ANY failure` comments, or not at all. `readFileB64` had no callers, and its `null` folded three conditions into one.

## Fix

- The five listings read `readdirMeasured`. A rejecting port is `unreadable`. In the population listing, a proven `absent` is an empty listing and asks no stat. An `unreadable` listing still asks `statMeasured`, as before, because an older agent folds every failure into `unreadable`, and the unmeasured verdict now names the reason. In the four switch reads, the pause or stop carries the reason: `CollectLaneSwitches`' `unlistable` arm gains `reason`, and the other three name it in their detail. No pause is narrowed or widened: every failed listing still stops the act, and nothing is deleted on a path that did not stop before.
- Every `FleetIO` member has a docstring with a `Failure contract:` line.
- `readFileB64` is removed from `FleetIO`, `localIO` and the remote io. `readFileB64Measured` remains, and its two round-trip tests now use it.
- A ratchet: the plain `io.readdir(` call sites under `server/src` may only go down. The count was 47 on `origin/main` and is 42 here.

## Tests

- `server/test/collect-temp-root-reads.test.ts`: a population listing measured absent is empty and asks no stat; one measured unreadable is unmeasured with its reason; a real mode-000 directory is reported unmeasured, never empty (skipped as root); the switches carry `reason` for unreadable, absent, a rejecting port and a real mode-000 registry. A characterization case pins today's verdicts for readable directories (it passes on `origin/main` too), and a separate case shows that the plain listing is never read.
- `server/test/end-dead-coordinator.test.ts`: the act and `stillCrashed` over a registry measured unreadable or absent, or whose listing rejects, stop and name the reason; a readable registry keeps today's verdicts.
- `server/test/expire-archived.test.ts`: a registry measured unreadable or absent, or whose listing rejects, defers, asks nothing, and names the reason.
- `server/test/fleetio-contract.test.ts` (new): the exact member list; a `Failure contract:` line on every member; `readFileB64` gone; the ratchet; none of the three coord files reads the plain listing.
- The collect fixtures (`collectLaneFixture.ts`, `collectTempRootFixture.ts`) intercept the registry on `readdirMeasured` and derive `readdir` from it, as `localIO` does.
