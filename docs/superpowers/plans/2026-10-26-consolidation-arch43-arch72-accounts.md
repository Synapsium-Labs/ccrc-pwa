## Review focus (the failure modes most likely to bite)

1. **A merged herd budget or a merged arming.** The two policies keep separate per-pass caps (`run-done`: 3 acts, 6 live measures; `idle-hours`: 3 acts, measures unbounded), separate arming (`run-done` has none and is live by D-5370, D-5427; `idle-hours` acts only with `hibernate-live`) and separate kill switches. One shared counter, or `idle-hours` reading `reclaim-paused`, is a behaviour change (spec K1: "Merging the rows' code never merges their arming or their herd budget").
2. **A hold that moved in the ladder.** The two ladders differ in order and in words (§C4a's difference table). `decideSleep` must answer each policy's word for every input, including two holds standing at once. A row that now answers the other policy's earlier hold is a regression even when both words hold.
3. **A boundary that slid.** `idle-hours` holds `recent` while the marker's quiet is **at most** N (`idleMs <= afterMs`); `run-done` holds while it is **less than** its threshold (`idleMs < grace`, or `STOP_DONE_PARKED_IDLE_MS` for a parked run or a sibling parked run). One comparison for both moves one millisecond of behaviour in one of them.
4. **A re-read at the act that went missing.** Both acts re-read inside the coordination serialiser and then the session's queue (serialiser outer): the registry record and the frame's row (`FleetWatcher#rowAtAct`), the store, presence and the live state, with only the roster falling back to the tick's read; `run-done` classifies `.hold` again at the act and passes the exact text to `--hold-ok`; `idle-hours` asks the verb again beside the argv. Losing any of these is the TOCTOU the retire lanes are pinned against.
5. **A word or a line that changed.** Hold texts (both maps), feed row titles and bodies (`Worker asleep`, `Workers kept awake by unread mail`), every `console.warn` line (`stop-done: put … to sleep (…)`, `hibernate-shadow`, `hibernated`, `hibernate-skip`, the two failure lines and their hourly throttles), the composed argv bytes and the declared actors (`sweep:stop-done`, `sweep:hibernate`) are pinned byte for byte.
6. **A switch the swap tick now writes differently.** C5b changes one statement inside `_auto_swap_check`: the rescue's staged write becomes `_authdead_mark "$wrapper" rescue-401 "$now"`. The epoch is the decision's `$now`, never a fresh `date`; the write still happens only under the same conjunction; nothing else in the function moves. Any other diff in `_auto_swap_check`, `_swap_idle_gate`, `cmd_swap`, `_swap_target`, the pool grammar or the strand path is refused in review.
7. **A frozen helper that was edited.** `ccd/ccd#_authdead_expired` is in the tick-diet harness's frozen closure (`ccd-tick-diet-equivalence-static.test.ts`, E0c: every live helper the vendored `_legacy_authdead` reaches must keep its base definition). It stays byte-identical; it is this plan's one named exception to the auth-dead primitive.
8. **A roster projection by the back door.** No generator, converge or account verb may derive `$REG/<w>-disabled` from the roster's kind fields (`homeAble`, `disabled` in a report). A hand-raised switch survives every verb that does not name its lane (spec K16).
9. **A lane that now waits on the other.** Today the tick fires `void this.sweepStopDone(…)` and `void this.sweepHibernate(…)` independently, each behind its own single flight. One `sweepSleep` must start each lane's `runLane` without awaiting the other: a `run-done` pass waiting on the coordination serialiser or a session's queue, or wedged under its `skip` flight, never delays or starves the `idle-hours` pass on the same tick, and the reverse. A `for … await` over `SLEEP_LANES` is a behaviour change.
10. **A remove that grew a stricter refusal.** `ccrc account remove`'s artefact loop refuses only a directory and removes any other object at `$REG/<id>-disabled` (a FIFO, a socket), with no postcondition. Enable's stricter test must not reach it (C5c's `on-any` mode).
