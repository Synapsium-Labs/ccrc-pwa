## Item 1: why W24-R12 was slow

**The cause.** The case called `knob()` once per setting, and each call is a fresh `bash -c '. ccd/ccrc; …'` that parses
the whole of `ccd/ccrc` (29,750 lines) again. `CCRC_SETTINGS` holds 99 names, so the case spawned 99 shells a platform,
198 for the pair. Its siblings R13 to R15 spawn one or two. Measured on the fleet box before any change: one source of
`ccd/ccrc` takes 27 ms; all 99 `_ccrc_knob` calls in one sourced shell take 156 ms.

**Why one shell decides the same.** `_ccrc_knob` clears `KNOB`, `KNOB_FROM` and `KNOB_WHY` on entry, keeps its working
values in locals, and re-runs `_box_unit_env` on every call that reaches it, so no call reads anything an earlier call
left. The case still unsets every setting first. Each answer now follows a marker line naming its setting, and the
markers must name every setting once, in `CCRC_SETTINGS` order.

## Item 5: corrections to c16.md and c17.md

These replace sentences in earlier notes, which stay as they are (as D-7282).

**c16.md, "Item 2 from 900 to about 1000 px"** (review 1723 N4). The heading says about 1000 px and the sentence under
it says about 960. One range, from the note's own figures: from 900 to 1000 px. At 900 the name is cut in all four
pairs of skin and pointer, and it is whole from 940 (Refined, a mouse), 960 (Instrument, a mouse), 980 (Refined,
coarse) and 1000 px (Instrument, coarse). Main was measured at 900 and 920 px only, where its name is whole, so "less
than main" is measured at those two widths; between 940 and 1000 px the comparison with main was not measured.

**c16.md, "For a real user"** (review 1725 F1). The reader focuses its frame once, right after it inserts the
`<iframe>`, and nothing is focused again at ready, while "Formatting…" stays up until ready. So only a focus the user
makes before the frame is inserted (while the reader's chunk and assets are still loading) is taken, not one made
later while "Formatting…" shows. The note's example, a Tab to back in the first moment of a page load, is right.

**c16.md, the proof** (review 1725 F2). "Every failing Tab went to the next stop after `<main>`" does not hold for the
two runs that failed "refined, 7 directories, 900 x 800" with an empty name. The case reads `textContent`, and an empty
one fits the reader's frame inside `<main>` (or an icon-only control), not the next stop after `<main>`. Read: the
other three failing runs' Tab went to the next stop after `<main>` ("Fleet", the status bar's key); the two empty-name
runs most likely left focus on the reader's frame, which is inferred from the code, not observed.

**c16.md, the proof** (review 1725 F3). "Three failed the focus-ring case" names one case where three different cases
of the four are meant: three runs failed a focus-ring case, Instrument at 1280 px and each skin at 768 px.

**c17.md, item 6, fix round 1** (review 1733 F2). (a) "The link is the arm whose behaviour changed" is too narrow. The
old `_reg_get` read a link, a directory and a mode-000 file alike as empty (rc 1), so the old forget went on in all
three, and `_reg_read_to` refuses all three (rc 2). All three arms changed; the link is the arm no case planted. (b)
"On reverted code the purge's removal" holds for a file or a link field, not a directory: the old `_reg_purge` runs
`rm -f`, which cannot remove a directory. The same sentence stood as a comment in `server/test/ccd-forget.test.ts` (the
case's `finally`); by the coordinator's ruling (mail 17122) that comment now says the purge removes a file or link
field, a comment-only change. No behaviour
depends on either sentence, and the guard (`existsSync(field) && lstatSync(field).isFile()`) is right for all three rows.
