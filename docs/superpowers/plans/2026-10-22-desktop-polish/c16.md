## Departures

- `refusal-gives-way-third` (item 2; was `refusal-grows-after-the-crumbs`, renamed in fix round 1 as review 1722 F2
  was ruled). The ruling names the refusal's ellipsis and small basis. A basis alone is not enough: a flex container's
  own width counts its items' max-content, so the actions still wrapped at the sentence's whole width. Item 2 therefore
  contained the words (`contain: inline-size`, floor `12ch`) and gave the crumbs a grow of a hundred thousand. That left
  the sentence at its 12ch stub ("View on Gi…", 86 px of 439) while the name and the action words showed, up to about
  1500 px for the longest reason (review 1722 F2). Fix round 1 applies the coordinator's order: when the row is short,
  (1) the action words hide, (2) the middle crumbs fold, (3) the sentence shortens to its 12ch floor, and (4) the
  page's name gives way, last. The containment holds only at the last stage, which `HeadRow` marks `data-fold`, and
  not when the user has unfolded the crumbs; before it the sentence is whole, and a row that cannot hold it whole with
  the name fails `rowFits` and moves on. Two costs, measured on the deep path: the crumbs stay folded up to 1420 to
  1480 px for the longest reason (1220 to 1260 for "no GitHub origin"), and the action words show only from 1400 to
  1420 for the short reason and not by 1600 for the longest. One order the ruling's list does not reach: the fold keeps
  the parent directory's crumb beside the name, and at the last stage the crumbs grow first, so that crumb is whole
  before the sentence grows past its floor (on the deep path, the longest reason sits at 86 px from about 960 to 1260
  px in Instrument under a mouse, the name whole). Putting the sentence ahead of that crumb but behind the name would
  need the sentence's whole width as a flex maximum, which `contain` hides from CSS, so a measured width; not done.
  Where the sentence is shortened, a touch screen shows no `title`; the Share sheet draws a refusal as text in a
  `role="status"` paragraph (review 1722 B3, which notes that `shareTargetFor` is commit-based and can disagree with the
  ref-based blob link). No touch-only mechanism was added. The sweep runs at 768 and from 900 to 1600 px; it is
  monotonic from 900 only (ruling 4 of fix round 1, and the Measurements below).
- `coarse-note-words-under-icons` (item 4). With its words drawn as a plain flex item (`flex: none`, about 270 px), the

the order they try is a pure fact, and it moved.
