# C8: one accounts poll, and an equal answer keeps the previous value (D-6440)

The task note for the programme ledger (Task C8 of `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-desktop-polish.md`). The
coordinator folds it into the ledger; this file edits no ledger row.

## What changed

- `pwa/src/stores/fleet.ts` is the one caller of `api.accounts`. `readAccounts` writes each of `roster`, `usage`,
  `lanes`, `accountsConnect` and the new `projected` only when the answer differs from what the store holds
  (`sameJson`), so an equal answer writes nothing. `projected` is `undefined` until an answer carries an object or
  `null`, and a malformed or failed read keeps the last good value.
- `AccountsStrip`, the Accounts screen's `useAccountsPoll`, `useProjectedHome` and `useAccountUsage` read the store
  through an optional store (`RunsScreen`'s idiom); `FleetCards` passes its chrome's store. Each keeps its signature
  and its inactive answer.
- `useFleetHealth` keeps the previous object on an equal answer; `useUpdatesView` keeps the previous view on an equal
  good read and still sets `seq` to that read's number (D-4273).
- One reader, one rule (intended change, in its own commits): a malformed `accounts`, `roster` or `projected` keeps
  the last good value in every reader. Before, the strip fell back to its placeholder (and crashed on a non-array
  `accounts`), the screen to its skeleton, `useAccountUsage` to `null` and `useProjectedHome` to `undefined`.

## Measured

Requests to `/api/accounts` per minute on the fleet screen, two sessions seeded:

| 1280 | Refined | 9 | 3 | 9 | 3 |
| 390 | Instrument | 6 | 3 | 6 | 3 |
| 1280 | Instrument | 6 | 3 | 6 | 2 |

Chrome counts requests in the 60 s after the first lane card draws, so a tick on the window's edge can fall either
side (the 2). Walking the fleet screen, the Accounts screen and the swap sheet cost 5 requests in Refined and 4 in
Instrument on `main`, and 1 with C8.

`sameJson` on a 17-lane answer (about 7 KB): 33 µs for the five field compares.
