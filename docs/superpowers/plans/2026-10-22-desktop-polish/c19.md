# C19 (D-8167): what the ledger should learn

A task note for the desktop-polish plan's Task C19 (the browser shards at their job limit), from its work run. It
records what the run measured and decided, so the coordinator can fold it into the programme ledger. It edits no ledger
row.

## Cause

In the merge queue, the `browser (pwa) 1/2` job was cancelled at its 20-minute job limit while its tests were still
passing. On 2026-11-10 that ejected one entry twice and cancelled two other groups: queue run 42805912574 (job
129794863321, the Test step ran from 11:15:34 until the cancellation at 11:35:17, its last files passing) and queue run
42800106251 (job 129775115106, cancelled 20 minutes after start). Over the green queue runs of 2026-11-09 and 11-10 the
two shards took 14 to 19 minutes each: pairs [18,16], [18,19], [16,16], [15,18], [18,14], [16,18], [18,17], [16,19].
D-7746 split the leg in two expecting about nine minutes a shard, with 47 files. The suite has grown to 82, and a loaded
runner tipped a shard past 20 whichever PR was in the group.

## What shipped

- **Three shards.** `ci.yml`'s `browser-pwa` matrix is `shard: [1, 2, 3]`, `total: [3]`. `timeout-minutes` stays 20:
  raising the limit is not the fix (the coordinator's ruling).
- **The job's comment carries the times.** Green queue run 42805910829 (jobs 129794853574 and 129794853772): 82 files,
  5,788 s of file time; the runner's Test durations 1,082 s and 982 s; jobs 18.6 and 16.9 minutes. Largest files:
  ask-inline 573 s, wheel-chain 355 s, wheel-chain-chip 248 s, runs-widths 246 s, frame-cost 243 s.
- **`FILE_MS` refreshed** (`pwa/browser/slowFirst.ts`) from the same run's logs. It now names all 82 files; 11 were
  missing and cost the table's median, runs-widths (246 s) among them, which skewed the two-shard split (3,001 s against
  2,787 s). Nothing in the sequencer reads the shard count: it arrives as `--shard=i/n`.
- **A pin on the count.** `server/test/ci-pipeline.test.ts` now pins `total` at 3, beside its existing pin that the
  shard list is exactly 1..total. Measured red against the two-shard matrix before the edit, green after.
