3. **What the watcher and its lanes reach: 165 of the 250 members.** A member is reached when (a) it is read on a store value in scope, whole or narrow; (b) a store value goes into a typed slot whose type is narrower than the whole store: the slot type's members. 6. **What that means for the port's shape (the ruling's boundary).** Every function on those chains must stop taking the whole class. A callee that the server's routes also call keeps compiling (a `CoordStore` is assignable to the port).
