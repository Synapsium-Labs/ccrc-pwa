# The disk-retention beats as lane-schedule rows

- **D-7895 (2026-11-03)** — the disk alarm (D-6615), the tmp sweep census (D-6624) and the stale-archive arm (D-6643) become rows of `LANE_SCHEDULE` (`diskAlarm`, `tmpCensus`, `staleArchive`), run by `FleetWatcher.runLane`, and their classes (`DiskAlarmLane`, `TmpCensusBeat`, `StaleArchiveBeat`) keep no clock or guard of their own. This finishes what the consolidation's move out of `watch.ts` (GitHub #1279) left as a follow-up (GitHub #1391). The one behaviour change is the clock each throttle reads: the wall clock (`Date.now()`) before, the watcher's monotonic clock (`Deps.monotonicMs`, else `performance.now()`) now, under OA-C4 (a) (ruled on #803), so a wall clock stepped back no longer holds a beat and one stepped forward no longer runs it early. Everything else is kept: the cadences keep their values (60 s, 5 minutes, an hour); each first pass is still at once (a monotonic row starts due); the disk alarm and the census still let passes overlap (`flight: 'none'`); the stale-archive arm's one-pass-at-a-time guard is now its row's `skip` flight, with no stuck-run age, as before; no row warns, because each pass keeps its own catch or hands its rejection to `tick`'s; and each pass body, its reads, writes, mail and the disk block's wire shape are unchanged, the verdicts still reading the wall clock. The three cadence constants move to `laneSchedule.ts`, the L0 table, which cannot import an L3 or L4 file, and their old homes re-export them (the `STOP_DONE_SWEEP_MS` precedent, D-7045). `laneClockReported` in `server/test/consolidationCensus.ts` now also names the three classes' old fields, and the pin list in `server/test/single-definition/advance-switches.test.ts` is empty and may only shrink. The rows are in `server/test/disk-retention-beats.test.ts` (fake monotonic time, a wall clock frozen or stepped, and a mutant row per beat).
