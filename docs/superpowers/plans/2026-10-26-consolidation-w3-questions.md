## Review focus (the failure modes most likely to bite)

1. **A swallowed question.** A deferred push that no carrier and no row can reach any more. The arms that guard against it are the missing-row push, the `unknown`-state push, the `answering` bound, the CAS-lost `held` keep, and `tryHold`'s compensation `staleAsk` after a half-committed mint. Each is a Q3a row.
2. **A push where there was none, or two.** A held question is recorded and never pushed until its window ends. A lapse pushes the mint's snapshot exactly once. A late answer replaces that push under the same tag. A cleared dialog pushes nothing.
3. **A principal that crossed.** No box-token request reaches the operator's arm, and no session-gated request reaches a claimant's arm. An alias decides nothing: its body is one delegating call.
4. **A changed word.** Every refusal string and code on every door, byte for byte.
5. **An unmeasurable read folded into an answer.** A store read that fails keeps today's direction on each path: the sweep retries, the operator's press goes through unrecorded, and a parent's answer refuses.
6. **A frame that changed for an old client.** The `ask` and `dialog` frames keep their send gates. `pairing` sits at the frame's top level, never inside `dialog`, so the old reducer's `{...s, dialog: msg.dialog}` carries nothing new.
7. **Two clocks merged.** The ask half keeps its 10 s throttle, and the approval half keeps running every tick.
