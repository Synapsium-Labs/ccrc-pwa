## Global constraints

The W0 plan's §Global constraints (public repository, SAFETY, no root runner, characterization discipline, counts measured, band routing, branch discipline) and the W1a plan's (each PR's first commit is a characterization pin, behaviour commits are their own, the act-time re-read stays, no `pwa/` file, no `ccd` byte, wire discipline, counted exits) bind this plan, with these additions. The one exception is C1l PR 2's `ccd/history/lib.mjs` (D-5867, §Deploy class).

- **Lanes stay methods** (O4 (a)). `runLane` owns the throttle, single-flight, catch and warn-once around a lane; it decides nothing a lane decides. A lane's decider, its executor and its act-time reads do not move in W1b.
- **Verdicts keep wall-clock.** Only a throttle or a single-flight may move to the monotonic clock. Expiry's `expiresAt`, stop-done's grace from `closedAt`, hibernate's marker stamps and every `now` a decider reads stay on wall-clock (spec K11).
- **Cadence is not safety.** A safety interval (the twice-observed window, a backoff base, a recovery retry) is never a `LANE_SCHEDULE` cadence column. A derived interval stays a declared reference to its source, never a second literal.
- **The act-time reads stay** (spec K1, K3, K12). C1j counts **selection** listings. A listing an executor takes at the act, or a lane takes as its last fail-shut switch read before it acts, stays fresh, is named in the census's act-read list, and is counted there, never folded into the tick's snapshot.
- **The egress redactor is a security boundary** (spec K7, D-5299, D-5360). Its output over the 196-row corpus (`server/test/fixtures/approvals/redact-corpus.json`) is unchanged byte for byte through every commit. No rule is unified across profiles in W1b: profiles whose rule for one shape differs (the URL-userinfo rule masks to the last `@` in egress and stops at the first `@` in docs) keep two rows.
- **No ring-crossing import.** `shared/redact.mjs` and `server/src/laneSchedule.ts` are L0 data and pure functions; `runLane` is L4 in `watch.ts`; `changeLatch.ts` is pure. The ring rules (D-6000) and the inner-ring host-import pin (D-6003) stay green.
- **Every new scanner uses `server/test/scan/parse.ts`** (D-5853). No suite here imports a parser.
- **`shared/redact.mjs` is browser-safe** (C1l): it imports nothing, names no host global (`process`, `Buffer`, any `node:` module) and runs nothing at load beyond building its table, because `shared/docs.ts` imports it and the PWA bundles `shared/docs.ts`. A CLI form for ccd's halves lives in its own file in W5, never in `redact.mjs`.
- **Deviation ledger.** This plan defines D-5861, D-5862, D-5863, D-5864, D-5866 and D-5867, one per task, and never allocates.

## Review focus (the failure modes most likely to bite)

1. **A throttle that moved clocks inside a move commit.** 23 of the 25 clocks throttle on `Date.now()` today, and 20 suites drive time with `vi.setSystemTime`. `runLane`'s `clock` column carries each lane's clock as it is today. Only the two behaviour commits change one (expiry and dead-coordinator first; the other 21 only after OA-C4), each with its before/after rows.
2. **A lost kick or a lost out-of-gate stamp.** `inventoryNow` and `serverCapsNow` stamp their clocks when they run on demand, and `childMarkLeftListing` resets `lastLifecycleSweep` to 0 so the next tick runs the lane. `runLane` keeps both as named operations (`stamp`, `kick`); a move that drops one changes when a lane runs.
3. **Skip, join, join with one rerun, abandon a stuck run.** The boolean guards skip a pass while one runs; `serverCapsRun` and `deadCoordinatorPassRun` hand the caller the run in flight; `inventoryRun` does that **and** queues exactly one rerun (`inventoryRerunPending`), which is the point of its trigger; `sweepPr`'s `prSweepStartedAt` skips while a sweep runs but abandons one older than `PR_SWEEP_STUCK_MS` and clears only its own stamp (`mySweep`). `runLane` has a flight mode for each, and each lane keeps its own. `dispatchNow` (`dispatchRun` plus one follow-up through `dispatchAgain`) has no cadence and stays hand-rolled in W1b, reported by name. `sealDeadCoordinatorBreakerNow` reads the dead-coordinator lane's in-flight run synchronously and awaits it; after the move it asks `runLane`'s `inFlight(name)`.
4. **A stamp moved after the await, or a re-stamp lost.** Child reclaim and collect stamp their throttle before the gather, so a tick that lands mid-gather is throttled, and **re-stamp it after the gather** with the decision clock (spec §5.7: otherwise two sightings can fall in one interval); child reclaim also drops a pass overtaken during the gather (`childReclaimPassSeq`). `runLane` stamps before it awaits the lane, for every lane, and exposes `restamp(name)` for the two lanes that re-stamp; the pass counter stays the lane's own.
5. **An act-time read folded into the snapshot** (C1j). `sweepMail`'s fail-shut `mail-disabled` read, `sweepAsks`'s `asks-disabled` read, and each executor's late read stay fresh at the act. The instrumented count separates selection listings from act-time reads, and a mutant that serves an act-time read from the tick's listing is red.
6. **A gate that changed polarity** (C1k). `verbSupported` permits on no evidence; `capSupported` refuses. Each `CCD_ARGV` row's `default` is today's polarity at every call site that gates that verb. A destructive verb (`ws-expire`, `ws-reclaim`, `ws-collect`) refuses on no evidence, as today.
7. **A redactor that over-masks or under-masks** (C1l). The egress corpus and the docs parity rows are byte pins; a rule moved into the wrong profile shows as a changed row, never as a passing suite.
8. **A gate that reads as ungated.** `verb-gate.test.ts` recognises a gate by the literal `verbSupported(`, and the docs adapter's functions by a literal `capSupported(` (`docs/ccdsource.ts`'s header). It learns `ccdCan(` in C1k's characterization commit, before any call site moves, and maps a `ccdCan` row key to its verb through its existing `VERB_OF`.
9. **A flag read as a verb** (C1k). `stop-surface` is a flag token: `server.ts#stopArgvFor` uses it only to choose `--surface`, and the `stop` verb is ungated by decision. A `stop` row gated `refuse` would refuse the operator's stop on every box without the token.
10. **A lane that moved inside the tick's await** (C1h). `tick` awaits `sweepHookStates`, `sweepUsage`, `detectDialogs` and `sweepTasks`, calls `sweepDigest` synchronously, and fires and forgets every other lane (`void this.sweepX(…).catch(() => {})`). `tick` keeps each lane's call shape: a fire-and-forget lane is `void this.runLane(…)`. An `await this.runLane('childReclaim', …)` would put a slow gather ahead of the dialog detector and the busy→idle push.
11. **A per-tick lane throttled by its inner prune** (C1h). `sweepApprovals` lapses held approvals, marks stale ones and releases deferred pushes on every tick; only its prune block is daily. Its row is `approvalPrune`, nested inside the lane, never the lane itself.
