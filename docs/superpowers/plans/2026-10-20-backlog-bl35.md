# Backlog bl35: declare a child's temp root, and correct the residue probe's rationale

**Issue:** #298. **Size:** S. **Deploy class:** a `shared/` manifest row and a `ccd/ccd` comment only; no behaviour, no wire change.

## Problem

Two of the issue's four items are mechanical.

- **Item 3.** The comment above `_ws_reclaim_residue` (`ccd/ccd`) said Claude Code lands its scratch "under whatever TMPDIR the ccd PROCESS runs under, never the child's own `$HOME/.cc-tmp/<id>`". The child-reclamation ledger measured the opposite (`docs/superpowers/programs/child-reclamation.md`, Measurement (a): "the scratchpad FOLLOWS `TMPDIR`"). The probe's root is still right for what it measures, which is a child spawned without `_child_tmpdir`'s TMPDIR (its rc 2). Only the rationale was wrong.
- **Item 4.** `shared/lifecycle.ts` had no row for `~/.cc-tmp/<id>`, though the artifact-lifecycle policy's ruling 4 first described that root.

Item 1 (an owner for loose top-level `~/.cc-tmp` entries as a class) waits on an operator ruling. Item 2 (trace the inflow) is optional and is not done here. The issue stays open for both.

## Fix

- The comment now cites Measurement (a). It says the child's scratch follows its TMPDIR into `$HOME/.cc-tmp/<id>/claude-<uid>/` and dies with that root. It says the probe's root holds the scratch of a child spawned without that TMPDIR.
- `shared/lifecycle.ts` gains `child-tmp-roots`: root `~/.cc-tmp/<id>/`, pattern S. Its creator is `_child_tmpdir`, and so is everything that follows TMPDIR in that session. Its collectors are the reclaim tail (`_ws_tmproot_remove`) and `ws-collect`, for a root that outlived its row. It is bounded by the child's lifetime. The row covers only the per-id leaf, so it claims none of the loose top-level entries.

## Tests

`server/test/lifecycle.test.ts`, in the policy §4(a) manifest block: a new case pins the row's name, root, pattern, creator, both collectors, its bound and its null ruling. This case is red at `origin/main` (no such class) and green with the row.

## Deviations and decisions

### Issued for this work and defined here

The row is the per-id leaf only, and loose top-level `~/.cc-tmp` entries stay undeclared until the operator rules on an owner (the issue's item 1).
