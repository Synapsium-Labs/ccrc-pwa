# The Overlord programme: the W0 and W1a plans, and their deviation ledger

The plans of the Overlord programme's first wave live in `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-10-overlord/`, one file per
piece: `w0-spike.md`, and the W1a foundations `w1a-mail-pace.md`, `w1a-skill-ratchet.md`, `w1a-roles.md`,
`w1a-time-ports.md`, `w1a-triggers-cases.md` and `w1a-simulator.md`. The spec is
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-overlord-design.md`, and the programme's ledger is
`docs/superpowers/programs/overlord.md`. This file is the index, and the one place the programme's deviation numbers
are defined. Each piece's plan states the full entry under its own "Deviations found".
