## What landed

- `pwa/src/fleet/delegationWords.ts`: `KEEP_WORD`, `ATTACH_WORD`, `NOT_DELEGATED_WORD`, `DISPOSITION_WORD` (total over every
  carrier state but `released`), `keptUntilWord` (the UTC day and month, the year only when it is not the clock's year),
  `keptWord`, `worktreeStateParts` (the disposition said once, with the date while held: "worktree kept until 14 Nov"),
  the readers `dispositionOf` and `expiryOf` (each delegating to the wire's one reader in `shared/api.ts`, so absence and
  an unknown word both read null) and `hiddenLeaseOf` (`leaseId`'s one reader), `leaseParentOf` (`via ?? parentId`, the
  lease's own parent under R9), `listedLive`, the controls `worktreeControls(l, liveParentLabel)` (an attach carries its
  one choice, and is offered only while that session is listed live) and `unownedControls(u)`, `controlName` (each
  control's name carries its worktree), the confirms (`attachConfirm`, which names no date; `notDelegatedConfirm`, whose
  consequence is the plan's sentence word for word; `confirmFor`), the done and verb words, and `REFUSAL_WORD` and
  `refusalWord` (D-8097).
- `pwa/src/lib/wireReaders.ts`: `readDoorAnswer` (a 2xx: `ok: true` with a string or null `expiresAt`, else `unreadable`)
  and `readDoorRefusal` (`{ok: false, error, detail}` with a string word), reading only the fields the PWA uses.
- `pwa/src/lib/api.ts`: `api.operatorRetainLease(leaseId)` and `api.resolveLease(target, body)`, both through one
  `doorPost` (`postJsonOr`, so a body that is not JSON never throws a parse), which resolves a refusal as an answer and
  rejects anything else. The 401 funnel is unchanged.
- `pwa/src/fleet/ProjectCard.tsx`: `WorktreeControls`, a leaf drawn in the worktree line's chip slot (so `WorkLine.tsx` is
  unchanged and names no broker word). Keep acts at once; Attach and Not delegated open a `QuickConfirm` (Not delegated as
  `danger` with Cancel focused), which keeps its words while it closes and is dropped when its row stops offering the act.
  An act takes the focus to the row's box, which holds it while the buttons are disabled; a row that leaves holding it hands
  it to its card's toggle. A refusal is said in a `role="alert"` region mounted empty; nothing is retried. A success
  raises a toast with the date; the row itself changes with the next frame.
- `pwa/src/fleet/fleet.css`: `.proj-work-controls` (tokens only, registered in `pwa/design/audit.mjs` and
  `pwa/test/role-census.test.ts`), and a bracket rule that keeps a wrapped work line's bracket on its words' first line.
- `pwa/browser/delegation-rows.browser.test.ts`: a newer server's fixture (a raw worktree with all three controls, an
  adopted one with its kept-until date, a hidden-lease unowned row, and one older-server row with none). Every width checks
  that each control sits inside its row and card on all four sides, is reached by a tap at its centre (never under the
  floating button), has a name of its own and, at 390, reaches the tap floor; and that every visible bracket sits on its
  words' first line, a wrapped line included.
