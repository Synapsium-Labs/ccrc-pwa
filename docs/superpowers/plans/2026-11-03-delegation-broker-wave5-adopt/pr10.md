## What landed

- `pwa/src/fleet/delegationWords.ts`: `KEEP_WORD`, `ATTACH_WORD`, `NOT_DELEGATED_WORD`, `DISPOSITION_WORD` (total over every
  carrier state but `released`), `keptUntilWord` (the UTC day and month, the year only when it is not the clock's year),
  `keptWord`, `worktreeStateParts` (the disposition word and, while held, the kept-until date, added to a worktree line
  only when the row carries a disposition), the readers `dispositionOf` and `expiryOf` (each delegating to the wire's one
  reader in `shared/api.ts`, so absence and an unknown word both read null) and `hiddenLeaseOf` (`leaseId`'s one reader),
  `leaseParentOf` (`via ?? parentId`, the lease's own parent under R9), the act rules `worktreeActs` and `unownedActs`, the
  two confirms (`attachConfirm`, `notDelegatedConfirm`, whose consequence is the plan's sentence word for word) and the
  done and verb words.
- `pwa/src/lib/api.ts`: `api.operatorRetainLease(leaseId)` and `api.resolveLease(target, body)`, both through one
  `doorPost`, which resolves a door's refusal (`{ok: false, error, detail}` with a `LeaseDoorRefusal` word) as the answer
  and rejects anything else. The 401 funnel is unchanged.
- `pwa/src/fleet/ProjectCard.tsx`: `WorktreeControls`, a leaf drawn in the worktree line's chip slot (so `WorkLine.tsx` is
  unchanged and names no broker word). Keep acts at once; Attach and Not delegated open a `QuickConfirm` (Not delegated as
  `danger` with Cancel focused). A refusal's detail stays on the row (`role="status"`); any other failure says the app's
  "Couldn't <verb> — <why>" there; nothing is retried. A success raises a toast; the row itself changes with the next frame.
- `pwa/src/fleet/fleet.css`: one rule, `.proj-work-controls`, tokens only, registered in `pwa/design/audit.mjs` (ground
  `var(--bg-surface)`) and `pwa/test/role-census.test.ts` (META).
- `pwa/browser/delegation-rows.browser.test.ts`: the fixture is now a newer server's (a raw worktree with all three
  controls, an adopted one with its kept-until date, a hidden-lease unowned row, and one older-server row with none), and
  every width checks that each of the six controls sits inside its row and card, and at 390 reaches the tap floor.
