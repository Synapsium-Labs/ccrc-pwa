  `keptWord`, `worktreeStateParts` (the disposition said once, with the date while held: "worktree kept until 14 Nov"),
  the readers `dispositionOf` and `expiryOf` (each delegating to the wire's one reader in `shared/api.ts`, so absence and

  lease's own parent under R9), `listedLive`, the controls `worktreeControls(l, liveParentLabel)` (an attach carries its
  one choice, and is offered only while that session is listed live; null for an older server's row, which has no
  controls at all, and `[]` for a row moving to a run, which has none for now) and `unownedControls(u)`, `offersControl`

  consequence is the plan's sentence word for word; `confirmFor`), the done and verb words, and `REFUSAL_WORD` and
  `refusalWord` (D-8097).
- `pwa/src/lib/wireReaders.ts`: `readDoorAnswer` (a 2xx: `ok: true`, else `unreadable`; `expiresAt` by the wire's one
  absence rule, so only a string is a date and absence or null is none known) and `readDoorRefusal(status, body)`, which
  reads a failed request as a door's answer in four parts (fix round 2, F2, the coordinator's ruling in mail 16314): a door
  word only at its own status; a word the route vocabulary names (`shared/refusal.ts#REFUSAL_STATUS`) that is no door word
  never; a word in neither, which only a newer server sends, only at a door's status, said as "The server refused this.";
  and a body without `ok: false` and a string `error` never. Only `ok`, `expiresAt` and `error` are read; the server's
  `detail` is not. It names the L0 identifiers `LEASE_DOOR_REFUSALS` and `isLeaseDoorRefusal`, which the scope census's
  regexes do not see (the census note below).
- `pwa/src/lib/api.ts`: `api.operatorRetainLease(leaseId)` and `api.resolveLease(target, body)`, both through one
  `doorPost` (`postJsonOr`, so a body that is not JSON never throws a parse), which resolves a door's refusal as an answer
  and rejects anything else (a 401, a 403, a 429, a 501 `not-configured`) to the app's `failedTo` words. The 401 funnel is
  unchanged.
- `pwa/src/fleet/ProjectCard.tsx`: `WorktreeControls`, a leaf drawn in the worktree line's chip slot (so `WorkLine.tsx` is
  unchanged and names no broker word). Keep acts at once; Attach and Not delegated open a `QuickConfirm` (Not delegated as
  `danger` with Cancel focused), which keeps its words while it closes, closes with them kept when its row stops offering
  the act or its session, and posts once: its confirm reads what it may post from a ref set at commit and refuses while an
  act is in flight (fix round 2, F1, F4). An act takes the focus to the row's box (a group named by the row's worktree,
  which draws no ring, being focused by script only); when the row's controls fall to none, or the row leaves, while the box
  holds the focus, it goes to the nearest heading still drawn: the group's toggle for a row in a delegation group (an
  unowned or offline-coordinator row), the card's toggle otherwise (fix round 2, F3; the ruling of round 1 said "the row's
  heading", and these toggles are the headings a row has). A refusal is said in a `role="alert"` region, an accepted act
  whose answer could not be read in a `role="status"` one, both mounted empty for as long as the row has controls at all,
  so neither unmounts while the row offers none; nothing is retried. The controls carry no tooltip of the row's (`title=""`
  ends its reach). A success raises a toast with the date; the row itself changes with the next frame. An older server's
  row mounts none of this, so it renders byte for byte as before.
- `pwa/src/fleet/fleet.css`: `.proj-work-controls` (tokens only, registered in `pwa/design/audit.mjs` and
  `pwa/test/role-census.test.ts`), and a bracket rule that keeps a wrapped work line's bracket on its words' first line.
- `pwa/browser/delegation-rows.browser.test.ts`: a newer server's fixture (a raw worktree with all three controls, an
  adopted one with its kept-until date, a hidden-lease unowned row, and one older-server row with none). Every width checks
  that each control sits inside its row and card on all four sides, is reached by a tap at its centre, has a name of its

  included; that, with every scroller at its end, every list button drawn whole in view, the page's last row of controls
