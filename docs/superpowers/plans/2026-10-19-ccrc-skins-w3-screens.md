## Global constraints

Every task's requirements include this section, and the foundations and W2 plans' Global constraints, which bind unchanged (public repository, no root runner, the palette is generated, the bridge maps and never defines, the role census, a skin owns no colour, one data model, one branch on the skin, a door runs its command, icons vendored as used, what a switch preserves, one instance per width, one derived value one function, landmarks and focus order, single-select is one choice, motion is tokened, the keyboard and the bottom edge, every door has a word, the tap floor, mutation discipline, the boundary tests, no new dependency, the deviation ledger, branch discipline, SAFETY). `CLAUDE.md` is the authority. W3 adds:

- **Public repository.** Every line, fixture, screenshot, commit message and PR body is public. Fixtures use neutral names (`project-a`, `lane-a`, `programme-a`, `ws-a`). ## Review focus (the failure modes most likely to bite)

1. **The list section lost or wrong.** Instrument's list column must keep the Inbox's feed while a row's session is open in the detail (spec §5.3: "the selected item's target opens in the detail pane"). A list section derived from the route alone switches the column back to the fleet table the moment a row is opened. Review P1a's rule for a route that names no section (`keep`), and what a reload does.
2. **Two instances of one list.** On Instrument at ≥900, `/inbox` must not render the feed in both the list column and the detail, and `/runs` must not render the run lists in both. P8 and P9 each count the mounted lists and the reads at 899, 900, 1599 and 1600.
3. **A branch on the skin in a shared screen.** A screen that calls `useSkin()` to decide whether to render its list forks behaviour. `useListColumnHolds` is the only question a shared screen asks (D-5469's census).
4. **The ack fired twice, or not at all.** Moving the feed into a reusable list must keep "opening this screen is the ack" exactly once per visit, and never on a mount that shows nothing (a phone route hidden by the pane swap counts as mounted in jsdom; the browser leg measures visibility).
5. **A tag that guesses.** `needsReason` answers `null` where no field explains a Needs-you row (D-5473, D-5497). The tag renders nothing then; it never defaults to `ASK`. `ALLOW` and `SIGN-IN` have no field on the wire and never render until one exists.
6. **The run number back on a card.** A fallback that prints `String(run.id)` or `#12` on a row breaks the card-glance ruling. Review every fallback branch of the run and archive rows.
7. **A route renamed without its alias.** An installed PWA and a bookmark still open `/mail`. It must land on the Inbox, by a `replace` (so Back does not bounce), never on `ScreenFallback`.
8. **A stepper that is a picture.** The wave stepper always carries its words (`wave 2 of 4`); a programme with no known wave count (`waveOf` null) shows the word alone, never a stepper of an invented length.
9. **The seam built for one screen.** W3-LC is used by four splits from three plans (P8, P9, A4, R3a). A registry keyed by anything but the list section (a route regex, a component name) will not serve Settings' six routes or a session opened from a list.
