# `registry-unmeasurable` gets one status, and its readers branch on the word (ARCH-11b, #549)

**Issue:** #549 (ARCH-11b, from the 2026-10-17 DDD, Clean Architecture and SOLID audit). It follows ARCH-11a (#547, D-5381), which put one status per route refusal word in `shared/refusal.ts`'s `REFUSAL_STATUS` and left this word as a measured split.

## The problem

`registry-unmeasurable` answered two statuses. `coord/routes.ts` and the table answer 502. `server.ts` answered 503 from eight sends in four routes: `/api/sessions/:id/kickoff`, `/stop`, `/swap` and `/archive`. The readers branched on the status, so the split showed on screen:

- `ArchiveConflictSheet` sent a 503 to "a reason this build does not recognise", and a 502 with no stderr to "the archive failed on the box". The box never ran the archive.
- `AbandonSheet` would send a 502 `registry-unmeasurable` with no stderr to "the release failed". The abandon route's close outcome does not send the word today, so its new arm is defensive.
- `ResumeSheet` read the registry sentence only off a 502.
- `CoordBanner` rendered a 502 inline but let a 503 fall through to the toast.

## The fix

- `server.ts`'s eight sends answer through `refuseRoute`, so the table's 502 is their status (D-5971). The literal-send pin for `server.ts` drops from 131 to 123, and `shared/refusal.ts` now lists two measured splits, not three.
- `pwa/src/lib/api.ts` gains `isRegistryUnmeasurable`, which reads the word. Each of the four sheets asks it before its status arms. `ArchiveConflictSheet` and `CoordBanner` render `apiErrorText`'s registry sentence. `AbandonSheet` renders ccd's stderr when it came with the word, and otherwise its new `registry-unmeasurable` sentence. `ResumeSheet` renders the door's `detail`, and otherwise its existing sentence.
- `ccd/ccrc-api` makes no status checks: it prints the body and the status and exits 0 on any HTTP answer. ccd's own status read treats 500, 502, 503 and 504 alike. Neither needed a change.

## The tests

- `server/test/refusal-status.test.ts`, a new `describe` block, "registry-unmeasurable has one status": the table says 502. No send of the word, in any file and in any shape (a literal or computed status, a header chain, or the word anywhere in the body), answers a status other than the table's. `server.ts` sends it only through `refuseRoute`. The predicate is live against a 503, a ternary and a header chain. The 503 split and the computed-ternary count are gone.
- The route suites that pinned 503 now pin 502: `routes`, `kickoff-route`, `archive-door`, `swap-route-busy`, `swap-route-pool` and `ccd-stop-argv-order`. So does `id-argv-census`'s identity-arm pattern.
- The four PWA suites gain a case for the word at 502 and at 503. The abandon suite also keeps ccd's stderr.

## Deviations and decisions

- **D-5971**: `registry-unmeasurable-one-status`. Every send of `registry-unmeasurable` answers `REFUSAL_STATUS`'s 502. `server.ts`'s eight sends go through `refuseRoute`, and `refusal-status.test.ts` holds that no send of the word uses another status. The PWA's four inline readers (`ArchiveConflictSheet`, `AbandonSheet`, `ResumeSheet` and `CoordBanner`) branch on the word through `isRegistryUnmeasurable` before their status arms, so a server still answering 503 during a rollout renders the same sentence.

Issued and unused: 5972.
