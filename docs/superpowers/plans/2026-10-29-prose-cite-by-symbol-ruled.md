- **D-7379 (2026-10-29)** — the row "Appendix A cites the presence constants" in `server/test/collect-lane-prose.test.ts` read two line numbers out of the child-reclamation spec's `shared/api.ts:NNNN-NNNN` citation and asserted the source lines at those numbers held `PRESENCE_REFRESH_MS` and `PRESENCE_TTL_MS`. Red first: with ten lines inserted above the constants, the old row failed (`expected '// shifted 0' to contain 'export const PRESENCE_REFRESH_MS = 15…'`) and the new row passed. No other prose pin under `server/test` checks a cited line number: the others read constants by a `^NAME=` regex, or match sentences.
