# Task C51: the coordinator badge sits on the title line (D-8834)

Task note for the desktop-polish programme (plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-desktop-polish.md`, section C51). Every claim is
labelled **measured** (a command or a real Chrome showed it), **inferred** (reasoned, not shown) or **record** (what a ruling, a mail
or the diff says; nothing to measure).

## The operator's look and the coordinator's ruling (2026-11-20) — record

C50 (#3111) put the coordinator badge where the word "coordinator" had stood, in the session header's details row. In the Instrument
skin that left the badge alone on a line of its own under the details, at 360 and 1280 px. The operator handed the look to the
coordinator, who ruled: in both skins the badge sits on the title line, right after the session's name, sized to the name's text and on
its baseline; the details row no longer carries it; the header loses the line the badge stood on alone. The badge itself is C50's
(`RoleBadge`, the network icon, the accessible name "coordinator", the role title on hover, the ink of the line it sits on); the fleet
row's role cell is unchanged.

## What changed (the whole branch) — record

- `HeaderTitleText` (`pwa/src/session/SessionHeader.tsx`), the title's own node in both skins, draws `RoleBadge` for a coordinator, glued
  to the name's last character. A worker's and a main checkout's title are as they were.
- The header view's `coordinatorRole` is `facts.coordinatorRole`, the fact the card already reads, so the role still has one reader.
- `headerMetaHasCells` (`sessionFacts.ts`) is the one reading of `SessionMeta`'s cells; `detailRows`, `detailsHaveFacts` and
  `detailsEndWithWorktrees` (`sessionHeaderView.ts`) decide the details row; `SessionDetails` draws from them.
- `chat.css`: C50's `.chat-fact-role` rules and the `min-height: 1lh` are removed; `.chat-title-glue` and `.chat-title-role` are added.
  No stylesheet rule reads whether the details row is empty.
- No server, wire, ccd or `@ccrc/ui` change.

## Why a box, and why the last character (measured on a probe page, one throwaway file, never committed)

A page of one `div` of 15 px monospace text, 241 widths from 90 to 330 px, two names (words, and one unbroken token), the badge as the
header draws it. Widths at which the badge sat alone on a line of its own, of 482: nothing glued 34; a word joiner (U+2060) before the
badge 34; a no-break space 31; an inline, not flex, badge 34; the last word and the badge in a `nowrap` box 0; the last character and
the badge in a `nowrap` box 0. The word joiner and the no-break space do not glue an atomic inline in this Chromium. **Inferred**: the
last character, not the last word, so a token wider than the column can still break inside itself (`overflow-wrap: break-word` has no
effect inside `nowrap`), as the ruling asks.

**The cost (record, and inferred as to the reason):** a coordinator's title is plain text, never a `TypedLabel`, because the label's one
text node and its `role="img"` root cannot hold a box that spans its last character and a sibling; a rename of a coordinator's name no
longer types in. It changes at once, as a main checkout's title does. For the operator if they want the typing back.

## The sweep (measured, real Chrome, Chromium 1243, through `ccrc test`)

`role-fact.browser.test.ts`, "the badge never sits alone on a line": in each of 12 views (two skins, two themes, 360, 390 and 1280
px) the name is replaced in the page by names of 1 to 130 characters, as words and as one token, 260 names a view. At every length:

- the badge's centre is inside the box of the name's last line: **green in all 12 views**; against the first version (an inline box with
  nothing glued) the sweep was **red in all 12**, with 4 to 46 names a view off the last line (for example Refined at 360 px: words
  of 29, 30, 67, 68, 108 and 109 characters, each 18.75 px taller; Instrument at 1280 px: 72 and 73 characters, 7.8 px taller).
- the header is no taller than the same header with the badge hidden (a worker's header of that name: the badge is the only difference
  on the title line, and a worker draws none) **wherever the glyph has room**, that is, where the name's last line ends at least 1 px
  before the column's edge less the glyph's 15 px and its 4 px gap. Where it does not, the header is taller by one line, and the
  glyph needs that line. **Measured**: 4 to 36 of the 260 names a view are such names (six distinct counts across the 12 views: 4, 9,
  15, 18, 30, 36), and the case allows no taller header with room. **The ruling asked "never taller than a worker's"; that cannot hold at
  those names, and this is the conflict between the rule and the task** (also in the plan section and the wave-done). A design that
  keeps it (the glyph in the gutter, or off the title) is a decision of its own.
- a worker is not rendered by the sweep; hiding the badge stands in for it (**inferred**: the badge is the only difference on the title
  line, which `session-line.test.tsx` and the worker cases of `coordinator-badge.test.tsx` and `header-details-facts.test.tsx` pin).

## The details row is decided in code (measured)

- `headerMetaHasCells` agrees with what `SessionMeta` draws for each of its 11 cells alone and for none (`header-details-facts.test.tsx`,
  21 cases). The spawn chip and the restart marker are the header's own chips, so a restart alone is no cell.
- With nothing to show: no `.chat-facts` and no Details door, for a worker and for a coordinator, in Refined and Instrument, on a phone.
  A session whose details are worktrees alone keeps the row and its door, and loses both with its last worktree. On a desk the row is
  drawn for its facts and absent without them.
- Focus (real Chrome, `delegation-rows.browser.test.ts`, the reviewer's probe: the lone session with its recorded entry and its context
  marker cleared, so its details are worktrees alone): when the last worktree leaves while its confirm is open, with and without a Tab
  inside the confirm, the focus rests on the header's title and the details row is gone, in both skins at 390, 1280 and 1920 px, both
  themes (12 cases, green). **A defect of mine found on the way**: a control registers its leave-time hand-off once, at mount, so it
  read the first render's value of the new flag; the flag is read through a ref now.

## Phone: no primitive shows a title on tap (measured by a search; inferred as to the reason)

The ruling asked whether `@ccrc/ui` already has a primitive that shows a title on tap. **Measured**: it has `Popover` (a Radix panel
opened by a trigger button, 12 rem wide at least, with its own focus scope), `Sheet` and `Confirm`; a search of `ui/src` for tooltip,
hint and long-press finds nothing, so there is none. Nothing is added. **Inferred**: making the badge a `Popover` trigger would turn a
mark into a 44 px control with a panel, a design decision the ruling did not make. The role title shows on hover only, as C50 left it,
and the accessible name "coordinator" is unchanged. A small tap-to-show primitive would be a task of its own.
