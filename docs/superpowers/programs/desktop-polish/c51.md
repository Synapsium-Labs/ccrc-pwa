# Task C51: the coordinator badge sits on the title line (D-8834)

Task note for the desktop-polish programme (plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-desktop-polish.md`, section C51). Each claim is
labelled **measured** (a command or a real Chrome showed it) or **inferred** (reasoned, not shown).

## The operator's look and the coordinator's ruling (2026-11-20)

C50 (#3111) put the coordinator badge where the word "coordinator" had stood, in the session header's details row. In the Instrument
skin that left the badge alone on a line of its own under the details, at 360 and 1280 px, a whole line of header for one glyph, and
a glyph that far from the name does not read as belonging to it. The operator handed the look to the coordinator, who ruled: in both
skins the badge sits on the title line, right after the session's name, sized to the name's text and on its baseline; the details
row no longer carries it; the header loses the line the badge stood on alone. The badge itself is C50's (`RoleBadge`, the network
icon, the accessible name "coordinator", the role title on hover, the ink of the line it sits on); the fleet row's role cell is
unchanged.

## What changed

- `HeaderTitleText` (`pwa/src/session/SessionHeader.tsx`), the title's own node in both skins, draws `RoleBadge` after the name when
  the session header view says `coordinatorRole`. In Instrument the title line reads "project-a › head-coord" then the badge; in
  Refined the project is the crumb above, and the badge follows the name on the title row.
- The view's `coordinatorRole` is `facts.coordinatorRole`, the fact the card already reads, so the role still has one reader.
- `SessionDetails` draws no role detail any more, and `Detail` loses the `className` only that detail passed.
- `chat.css`: C50's `.chat-fact-role` rules and the `min-height: 1lh` are removed. `.chat-title-role` sets `vertical-align:
  -0.125em` (the icon rule), the crumb separator's gap and `svg { width: 1em; height: 1em }`, and no font size and no colour, so the
  glyph follows the title's text size and the line's ink is the title's.
- One more rule, `.chat-facts:not(:has(> :not(.sess-meta:empty))) { display: none }`: a coordinator with no other fact has a details
  row holding only `SessionMeta`'s own empty `.sess-meta`, and an empty full-width row still costs the meta block's row gap.
  **Measured**: without the rule the header lost 16.5 px of the line's 21.5 px (row 16.5 px plus a 5 px row gap) in Instrument.
- No server, wire, ccd or `@ccrc/ui` change. The phone question is below.

## What the placement measures (measured, real Chrome)

- The glyph is the name's font size in both dimensions (the title's `--fs-base`; 15 px in the mutant run's message), its centre is inside the box of the name's last
  line, and its bottom edge is on the text baseline lowered by no more than the icon rule's 0.125 em (a zero-size inline-block probe
  beside the badge gives the baseline).
- Hiding the badge changes the header's height by 0.5 px or less in every view: it adds no line of its own.
- Instrument at 360 px: the header, with its details open, is 160 px tall where it was 182 px (screenshots). The probe that rebuilds
  C50's row (a flex line of 1 lh at the details' font size, the glyph 1 em of `--fs-meta`) costs the header 16.5 px for the row and the
  5 px row gap, 21.5 px in all, and the text block loses exactly that.
- Instrument at 1280 px: 85 px before, 65 px after (screenshots). The probe measured the header losing 19.25 px, 2.25 px less than
  the line, while the text block loses it whole (the case asserts the block within 1.5 px of 21.5). **Inferred**: the keycap
  column (Terminal, More, Check PR) is the taller of the header's two columns by that margin, and its height is a floor. Not shown by a
  separate measure.
- Refined: unchanged heights, 109 px at 360 (the rail does not wrap there) and 85 px at 1280, details open or closed. The badge
  shared a rail row with the other chips before, so moving it frees no line there.
- Phone, details **closed**: the badge is drawn on the title line, where C50's was hidden behind the details door until it was
  opened (the before and after `-closed` screenshots). The header's height is the same, 160 px Instrument and 109 px Refined.
- Real Chrome, the other header files, all green: `session-header` (76), `instrument-desk` (33), `goals-door-height` (28), and
  `role-fact` whole.

## Phone: no primitive shows a title on tap (measured by a search, inferred as to the reason)

The ruling asked whether `@ccrc/ui` already has a primitive that shows a title on tap. **Measured**: it has `Popover` (a Radix panel
opened by a trigger button, 12 rem wide at least, with its own focus scope), `Sheet` and `Confirm`; a search of `ui/src` for tooltip,
hint and long-press finds nothing, so there is none. Nothing is added. **Inferred**: making the badge a `Popover` trigger would turn a
mark into a 44 px control with a panel, a design decision the ruling did not make. The role title shows on hover only, as C50 left
it, and the accessible name "coordinator" is unchanged. A small tap-to-show primitive in `@ccrc/ui` would be a task of its own, for the
operator to ask for.
