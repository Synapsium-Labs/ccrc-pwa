## Choices made in the wave, for the ledger

| Choice | Why |
|---|---|
| The offer is one component, `pwa/src/docs/artifact/ArtifactOffer.tsx`, placed six times | One owner of the offer's markup; it takes one census row of its own instead of riding `MockupView`'s and `DocsLeaf`'s |
| One module store (`useArtifacts.ts`) for the availability, the live list and which page's sheet is open | The offer, the sheet and both lists read one answer and refresh together; `MockupView` remounts on a draft's fingerprint, and the open sheet must survive it |
| The sheet and the mint share one admission function, `originAdmitted` in `shared/artifacts.ts` | The sheet's first filter was a second copy of the mint's rule and had already drifted; consolidate, don't duplicate |
| A held capability whose Open was never tapped is revoked on a tick change, a pin change, an unmount or a departed origin; once Open was tapped it lives on | A link nobody received should not stay live; a tab the operator opened is the lists' to end |
| The census is `'pending'` until the walls' load answers, apart from `null` (not scanned) | No overloaded null at a seam: Prepare is held while pending, and a page never scanned mints with no outside host |
| D-9161's predicate is the shape half of the answer mask (`matchesShape`), handed in by `loadConfig` | `artifactConfig` stays pure L1, and the L4 masker is not imported by the L3 config (rings) |
| The browser suite's console is a fixture page with the sheet's link; the sheet's own `rel` is pinned in jsdom (MX5.39) | The opener case cannot go red for the `rel` alone (D-9164) |
