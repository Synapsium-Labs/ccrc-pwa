Desk navigation mockup

A sketch of docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-desk-nav-design.md, not the product. Instrument skin, fixture data only: every name is a stand-in. Colours follow the console's Phosphor & Ink palette; icons are the console's vendored Lucide set. Each frame is drawn at its real pixel size and scaled to fit.

Part A, the top bar. The rail's doors move into the top bar where the breadcrumb was. The wordmark becomes the way home to Fleet. Bell and Settings move to the bar's right end. The main pane gains the rail's 52 px.
Part B, the Docs head. Back, crumbs, Aa and the three actions share one row; the truth line is one dense line under it. The title moves from y = 382 to about y = 200 at 1280 × 800.
Marks. An orange dashed outline is what Part A removes (the rail and the breadcrumb). An orange dashed line across a Docs frame is the document title's y.
