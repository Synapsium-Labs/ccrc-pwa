ccrc Design System Audit

Where the design system is strong, where it is thin, and the eleven things worth doing next. All of it is now done: eight landed, one refused with a measurement, and the two that change how the app looks were ruled on and landed too.

31 components 166 tokens 12 palettes 3456 contrast assertions 3967 tests
8.5 / 10

Unusual shape for a design system: the guard layer is better than most commercial ones, and nearly every weakness is a coverage gap rather than a correctness gap. Nothing here is wrong. Several things are simply absent.

What is genuinely good

The guard layer is the product

Six censuses, each written after measuring that the previous one was blind to a real copy, each carrying a mutation table in its own header. This is the repo's "a comment is a request; a red suite is a mechanism" doctrine actually executed rather than quoted.

shape-census argues its 72% threshold from measurement — 0.80 loses .program-start-door, 0.65 fills with noise. literal-census was written after a scan found 17 duplicated sentences, one of them in seven files, three of which carried a comment claiming it was not a second copy.

The token-to-Tailwind bridge is guarded against its own two historical failures

Both are invisible to the contrast gate, because they are declarations rather than rules: @theme without inline bakes one palette's value, and a self-referential alias silently drops the token. Twelve of those shipped once.

pwa/test/theme-bridge.test.ts — the only thing standing between the system and a repeat.

The contrast gate is real and exhaustive

3456 assertions across 12 palettes, every text-on-ground pair scraped from both packages' stylesheets, all passing. And the stylesheets argue in token terms rather than in hex.

chat.css explains why --ink-on-well rather than --ink-secondary: 2.44:1 on the well, measured.

control-census answers a question almost no design system asks

Not "does the primitive hit 44px" but "of the 48 controls the app draws by hand, how does each one reach the thumb" — with four verified answers. The UNDER arm is checked in the negative, so a control that quietly gains the token reds and demands re-classification. A preserved defect does not get to keep its excuse.

30 TOKEN · 6 SELECTOR · 6 UNDER · 2 OVERLAY · 2 INLINE · 1 VARIANT

TextInput refuses a size variant, and is right to

"A size="sm" prop here would be a loaded gun pointed at mobile." Below 16px, iOS Safari zooms the page on focus. The refusal is the feature.

ui/src/primitives/text-input.tsx — --fs-input: 16px, no size axis.

Where it is thin

The design system cannot render a dropdown

The same control is written in two files, held in agreement by nothing but a shared class string. Settings hand-rolls radio inputs whose tap floor lives on the wrapping label; the caps dial is a third shape. There is no Select, no Radio, no Checkbox.

pwa/src/fleet/NewSessionSheet.tsx:92 and pwa/src/screens/FleetScreen.tsx:341 — both <select className="route-select">

fleet.css is 3287 lines

64% of the app's CSS, and more than twice the entire design system's component CSS. The censuses stop a new duplicate landing; nothing shrinks what is already there. The registry of registered duplicates is, in effect, the list of what to delete next.

380 rules in fleet.css against 245 rules / 1527 lines across 13 files in ui/. shape-census currently registers 16 duplicate clusters, 8 of them app-internal.

Architecture shaped by a test's key format

Five duplicate clusters between mail-strip.css and task-strip.css survive because hoisting them would rekey every contrast-gate entry naming the old sheet. The shape has already migrated to CollapsibleStrip; only the skin stays twinned. This also blocks the Select primitive.

The gate's rule key is <basename> <selector>, so moving a rule between sheets moves its key.

Nothing in CI ever renders a component

ui/'s test script is a typecheck. The a11y addon is installed and dev-only. Vitest runs with css: false, and the contrast gate scrapes stylesheets rather than computed styles — so if bg-raised fell out of the button variant, every quiet control in the app would render transparent and the whole suite would stay green. quiet-control.test.ts says so in its own header.

Storybook builds and is thrown away. ci.yml names the missing piece: a test runner and a browser, which is a real dependency decision rather than a line of yaml.

What the work found that the audit could not

Four defects, none of them visible to a reading of the source — which is what the audit was. Each is fixed and each has a mechanism behind it now.

cn was eating utilities on the way to the DOM

tailwind-merge classifies an unknown utility by PREFIX, and this theme renames two families Tailwind v4 owns — so text-input and text-2xs read as text COLOURS and font-regular as a font FAMILY. Seven shipped class strings lost a utility: TEXT_INPUT and SELECT rendered with no ink utility at all, Well and ControlRow in the UI family rather than mono. Every guard in the tree reads the class STRING, and the string was right.

The new guard reads the composed CONSTANTS, not the per-file literals, because that is the only source that can see it: TEXT_INPUT is five literals with comments between them, so the two colliding utilities never meet in one literal. Deleting either group from cn.ts is 1 red; dropping the constant source is green, which is the finding.

A scrollable <pre> no keyboard could focus

Well is max-h-well-max plus overflow-y-auto — it scrolls by construction — and nothing could focus it, so a keyboard could not read past the fold of a long tool output. A mouse wheel and a finger always could.

axe scrollable-region-focusable, through the Storybook runner, on the Tall story. Found the first time a browser rendered this package.

The accessibility tree was dropping TypedLabel's whole purpose

The component exists to hold a stable accessible name while the visible text animates — its header argues that at length. The name was on a roleless <span>, where ARIA forbids it, so it was being discarded: the fleet line's button announced the typing animation after all.

axe aria-prohibited-attr. role="img" is the smallest role that both permits a name and tells a screen reader to announce it instead of walking the children.

Two stories modelled the app's own call sites wrong

A nameless Keycap (all six real call sites pass aria-label) and a CoverScreen dialog with no name (LoginScreen does it right with aria-labelledby). A story that renders a nameless button teaches the next call site to write one — and the design system's documentation was the thing teaching it.

axe button-name and aria-dialog-name.

Two that needed your ruling — both ruled, both landed

Both change how the app looks, so neither was a refactor's call to make. The ruling was: do both. Four controls left the census's UNDER arm, which is down from six to two.

V1  ·  Grow the two session-header meta chips to 44px  landed

At roughly 19px visible they were the smallest controls in the app, floored to 24px for one wave — and both are live choosers, not labels. The census had already proved an overlay cannot rescue them, because it would overhang the sibling chip and an overhang between two live choosers opens the wrong one.

The floor went on button.metachip rather than on .metachip, and that is what makes it safe: .metachip--branch is a <span> stating the branch and keeps its 24px box. A control gets the target; a label does not.

Landed: the session header on every screen, ~20px on one meta row.

V2  ·  Grow the seen-bucket and compaction headers to 44px  landed

24px and roughly 29px, both argued at length in the stylesheet as deliberate WCAG 2.5.8-only choices, and the last wave deferred them on purpose: 44 adds about 15px to every compaction marker in a transcript.

The ruling weighed that against the axis a thumb actually misses on — a full-width disclosure is easy to hit horizontally and was short only vertically — and against an ack that cannot be undone. Both arguments survive in the stylesheets, now as history rather than as a deferral.

Landed: every transcript, and the fleet's bucket bars.

Already settled — do not re-open

Each of these is a mechanism that exists, is mutation-tested, and runs in CI. Adding a second one is churn.

  • Reduced motion — motion-reduced.test.ts; it knows transition does not inherit
  • Tap floor — tap-targets.test.tsx plus control-census, whose UNDER arm is down from six controls to two since V1 and V2
  • Duplicate CSS, markup and copy — shape-census, markup-census, literal-census
  • Theme aliasing and palette drift — theme-bridge, theme-catalogue, themes:check
  • A utility lost inside cn — utility-pairs.test.ts, over the composed constants
  • A selector declared by two sheets — audit.mjs, now that the gate's key is the selector
  • What a component actually RENDERS — storybook-a11y, 152 stories through headless chromium
  • RTL — deliberately not pursued. Three logical properties against about 26 physical ones, on a single-operator English console. Cost with no reader.
