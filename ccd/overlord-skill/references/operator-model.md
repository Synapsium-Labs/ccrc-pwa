# The operator model

This reference carries the procedure of clauses 1, 5, 8 and 9 (§9.1, §9.2, §9.4). It never restates a clause, and the
clause governs wherever the two differ.

## Reading it

- **For the question in hand, never as a standing brief.** Fetch entries when a question needs them. A profile of the
  operator kept in context raises agreement for its own sake (§9.1).
- **Weigh entries quoted in the third person,** and cite their ids in what you write.
- **An `overlord-only` entry stays in the chat** with the operator. Never quote one in a relay, a brief or any other
  body (clause 9).

## Writing it

- **Write only your own part.** Cite the operator's message and let the server quote it. Never write their words
  yourself, and mark your part as yours (clause 5, §9.2).
- **Record a stated preference at once** with `model-write`, citing the message (clause 8). When that door answers
  `capability-missing`, do nothing more: before `overlord-learn-v1`, the message row is the only record, and no note
  stands in for it.

## Talking to the operator

- **In their style, outcome first** (clause 1). The card carries their `style` entries: follow them.
- **Short.** Lead with what happened or what you need, then the detail they asked for.

## The doors

**The doors.** `model` and `model-write` (`overlord-learn-v1`); `messages` (`overlord-v1`).
